scope gloss, matching the other four `bc_traces` comment lines' convention
of glossing the traced BC's own core postcondition, not a laundry list of
neighboring BCs.

**Remediation:** placement note L15 corrected to
`# downstream ARQ (piggybacked ACK/SACK)`, matching the story's GAP-1 form.

### Finding-2 (F-R33-LENSB-02, LOW) — AC-002(b) / BC-2.01.003 Anchors Consumed row overclaim

AC-002(b) and the BC-2.01.003 Anchors Consumed row (added at v1.19) stated
that AC-002's tests "exercise" BC-2.01.003's sequence-space-independence
aspect. This overclaims: only clock/interval independence is actually
test-exercised (`TestLoopbackDriver_TicksFireOnSchedule`); sequence-space
independence is a by-construction property — each direction owns its own
`halfchannel.New()` instance and a private `seq uint32`, and
`multipath.Frame` carries no cross-channel sequence field — not itself
asserted by any test.

**Architect adjudication: FIX.** Precision matters for an anchor row's
discharge claim; "test-exercised" and "true by construction" are different
verification strengths and should not be conflated.

**Remediation:** AC-002(b)'s parenthetical and the Anchors Consumed row's
trailing clause both tightened to state the two properties separately and
precisely. No new AC (AC-002 wording tightened in place); no new BC trace.
